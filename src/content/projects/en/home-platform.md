## Problem

The home.pl frontend team builds the marketing and sales sites of a multi-brand hosting group: home.pl, az.pl, strefa.pl and IONOS in Romania, Bulgaria, Czechia, Slovakia and Hungary. Every brand needs landing pages, offers and sales paths, and the pages are put together by editors, not developers.

## What we built

- **Content in a headless CMS, pages built by Gatsby.** Almost everything on the sites is stored in the CMS and generated by Gatsby through GraphQL.
- **A generic page creator.** A colleague and I worked it out at the beginning: editors compose a page in the CMS from a set of predefined sections, and Gatsby builds it for every brand.
- **More than 700 landing pages across 8 brands**, plus the sales paths behind them: upsells, baskets and product configurators.
- **A new CMS and more brands.** In 2019 there was one IONOS brand and the content lived in Contentful. Since then it has moved to Squidex, and the group has grown to 8 brands.
- **A frontend library in transition.** The library on styled-components was already there in 2019, together with Gatsby; we used it and extended it for years. Now we are moving to Tailwind and our own library built from Figma tokens, with Storybook and multi-brand theming, the same approach as in my [design system](../design-system/).

## Highlights

- Editors, not developers, put the pages together: they pick predefined sections in the CMS, and Gatsby builds the page for every brand.
- The sales paths are part of the same system: upsells, baskets and product configurators on the way from a landing page to an order.
- I onboarded new developers into the project.
- This system is the ground my AI work stands on: the [QA agent](../qa-agent/) and the [human-in-the-loop delivery](../delivery-loop/). The delivery runs on my "resolve ticket" skill, which reads Figma and builds components and sections in Tailwind step by step.
